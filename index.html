Case study · UScellular “US Mode” · Summerfest

A 15-foot boombox.
648,112 presses.

At Summerfest, “US Mode” asked festivalgoers to do one thing: put the phone down — literally, into a slot on the podium. That started 45 seconds on a wall of 40 backlit buttons: blue for healthy digital habits, points up; red for distractions, points down, with a giant radio-dial screen scoring every press live. Pivot engineered the whole game to run on local hardware — Raspberry Pi driving the buttons and lighting — so it never waited on festival internet, and it reset in under ten seconds to keep the line moving all day. Scores turned into prizes on the spot.
